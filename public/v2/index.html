Why Join AOG

Ownership is the
whole model.

At AOG, we believe the future of financial services belongs to those who own it. Our innovative model unites Affiliates—insurance agencies, broker-dealers, RIAs—and Vendors—including providers, support firms, and revenue share companies—under a shared equity structure.

Together, we're building a stronger, more connected financial services ecosystem—one where growth and ownership go hand in hand.
