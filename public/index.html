01 The Thesis

The hero is no longer just a producer, but an owner.

Associate Owners Group exists to fundamentally realign the financial services ecosystem. We believe the professional who creates the value should share fully in the value created — not only through their commissions, but through ownership across the entire economic stack.

AOG is building a platform where the “hero” is no longer just a producer, but an owner participating in the front and back end of every transaction.

This is not simply a better compensation model. It is a structural shift in how wealth is created:

  1. FromIncome→ToOwnership
  2. FromIndividual production→ToShared enterprise value
  3. FromFragmented effort→ToAligned participation

02 Four Pillars of Ownership

The architecture underneath every partner.

Spanning insurance, wealth management, technology, and products and services — the structural foundation on which the AOG family of companies is built.

PILLAR 01

Insurance

We acquire and operate agencies while preserving the books, the relationships and the culture that built them — and give the producers who wrote the business a stake in what it becomes.

PILLAR 02

Wealth Management

Advisory and retirement practices gain the capital, custody relationships and planning depth of a national platform, while the advisors who earned the trust retain ownership in the enterprise.

PILLAR 03

Technology

A unified stack — CRM, quoting, servicing and data — built once and deployed across every firm in the family, so Associates spend their time selling rather than reconciling systems.

PILLAR 04

Products & Services

Preferential terms with leading carriers and providers, collective bargaining power and regulatory representation that no single agency could secure on its own.
