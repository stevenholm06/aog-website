The Model

Ownership isn't a perk.
It's the structure.

At AOG, we believe the future of financial services belongs to those who own it. Our model unites Affiliates — insurance agencies, broker-dealers, RIAs — and Vendors, including providers, support firms and revenue share companies, under a shared equity structure.

Together we're building a stronger, more connected ecosystem — one where growth and ownership go hand in hand.

01

Ownership & Equity

Partners share in the upside. Equity flows to the people producing revenue, not only to those managing it.

02

Unified Growth Strategy

Collaboration across a powerful network — agencies, advisory firms and vendors compounding each other's reach.

03

Industry Support

Operations, technology, compliance and marketing built once and shared across every company in the family.

Four Pillars of Ownership

The architecture
beneath every partner.

Spanning insurance, wealth management, technology, and products and services — the structural foundation on which the AOG family of companies is built.

01

Insurance

We acquire and operate agencies while preserving the books, relationships and culture that built them — and give the producers who wrote the business a stake in what it becomes.

02

Wealth Management

Registered advisory and retirement practices gain the capital, custody relationships and planning depth of a national platform, while advisors retain ownership in the enterprise their assets fund.

03

Technology

A unified stack — CRM, quoting, servicing and data — built once and deployed across every acquired firm, so producers spend their time selling rather than reconciling systems.

04

Products & Services

Preferential terms with leading carriers and service providers, collective bargaining power, and regulatory representation that no single agency could secure alone.
